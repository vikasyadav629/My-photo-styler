FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I send my photos for editing?
+
Use our Upload page — drag and drop your files, choose your service, and write your editing instructions. We accept JPEG, PNG, RAW, MP4, MOV and more.
How long does photo editing take?
+
Standard turnaround is 24–48 hours for photos and 48–72 hours for videos. Rush delivery is available for an additional fee.
Are revisions included in the price?
+
Yes — all packages include at least 2 free revisions. Premium packages include unlimited revisions.
Do you keep my photos private?
+
Absolutely. Your files are stored with encryption and deleted 30 days after delivery. We never use your photos without written permission.
How do I pay? Do you accept USD and EUR?
+
Yes — we accept USD and EUR via PayPal, Wise, and bank transfer. Invoices are issued before work begins.
